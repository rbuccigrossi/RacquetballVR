Prepare three alignment marks

Facing the intended front wall, mark A at the rear-left corner of your physical footprint. Mark B exactly 1 meter right of A, and C exactly 1 meter forward of A. Use the same controller reference point at all three marks.

Hold the center of the right controller grip 10 cm above each mark, using a measured support for repeatability. Press its trigger and hold still for half a second. Both players sample A, B, then C. Remove the support from the play area afterward.

Join the sandbox, then enter VR to align.
